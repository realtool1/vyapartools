Understanding Stacked Sequential Discounts

Multiple discounts applied sequentially are not additive. A 20% discount followed by a 10% discount is not equivalent to a flat 30% reduction.

The secondary discount applies to the price remaining after the primary discount has been deducted. For instance: ₹2,000 - 20% = ₹1,600, and ₹1,600 - 10% = ₹1,440 (Effective total savings of 28%).
