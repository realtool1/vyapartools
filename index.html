Frequently Asked Questions

What is VyaparTools?

VyaparTools is a suite of free business utilities designed to assist shopkeepers, freelancers, and entrepreneurs with fast operational calculations.

Are calculations stored or logged on servers?

No. All calculations run strictly client-side inside your web browser. We do not capture or record your financial entries.

What is the difference between Margin and Markup?

Margin represents profit as a percentage of total revenue/selling price. Markup measures profit relative to unit cost price.
