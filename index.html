Frequently asked questions

Kya VyaparTools bilkul free hai?

Haan. Saare calculators aur invoice generator 100% free hain. Na signup, na credit card, na hidden limit.

Mera data kahin save hota hai?

Nahi. Har calculation aapke browser me hi hota hai. Invoice generator ka data aapke device ke local storage me save hota hai — server par kuch nahi jaata.

GST calculator kaunse rates support karta hai?

0%, 0.25%, 3%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% aur custom rate bhi. Inclusive (reverse) aur exclusive dono modes available hain.

Margin aur markup me kya difference hai?

Margin profit ka percentage hai selling price par, aur markup profit ka percentage hai cost price par. Dono ka apna use-case hai — hamara Profit Margin Calculator dono dikhata hai.

Invoice ka PDF kaise banaun?

Invoice generate karke Print button dabaiye, phir print dialog me "Save as PDF" choose kijiye. Bas.
