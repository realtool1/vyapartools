Understanding Margin vs Markup

Although both metrics analyze your profit, they evaluate business health differently:

Profit Margin: Percentage of selling price that translates into profit. Margin = (Profit / Selling Price) × 100

Markup: Percentage added to cost price to determine selling price. Markup = (Profit / Cost Price) × 100
