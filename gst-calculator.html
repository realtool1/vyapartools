How GST Calculation Works

Add GST (Exclusive): Used when your price does not include tax. The tax amount is calculated directly on the base figure and added to get the final payable sum.

GST Amount = (Base Amount × GST Rate) / 100

Remove GST (Inclusive): Used when your gross total includes tax. This formula separates the tax portion to determine the actual net base amount.

Base Amount = Gross Amount / (1 + (GST Rate / 100))
